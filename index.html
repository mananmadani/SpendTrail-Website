A money tracker that never leaves your phone.

SpendTrail records your income and spending, draws the charts and writes the PDF statements, all inside your browser. No account, no server, no ads.

Open SpendTrail Read the source

Free under the MIT licence. Version 4.0.17, November 2026, now with dark mode. Runs on Android, iPhone and desktop.

Four screens do most of the work.

Log a payment, see where the money went, look up an old entry, and keep home and work separate. Everything else stays out of the way.

Log a payment in a few taps.

Enter an amount, a category and a date, and add a note if you want one. Categories you have used before are suggested as you type, and your balance updates the moment you save.

  • Expense and income share one quick form
  • Long-press any entry to edit or delete it

See where it went.

A doughnut chart splits spending by category, bars rank every category, and a trend line shows the last 7, 30 or 90 days. Switch to income with one tap.

  • Small categories group into "Others", which you can open up
  • Charts follow your light or dark theme

Find any entry later.

The ledger groups everything by category. Search by category or note, filter to income or expense, and open a category to see every entry in it.

  • Build a custom statement for any date range
  • Export it as a colour-coded PDF with totals

Keep home and work apart.

Create up to five profiles, such as personal, business, travel or family. Each has its own entries and its own currency, and deleting one never touches the others.

  • Pick light or dark under More, Appearance
  • Back up one profile at a time, plain or encrypted

And the details you would ask about.

Works offline

After the first visit the app is cached on your device. Add entries on a plane or in a basement shop.

30 currency symbols

₹, $, €, £, ¥ and 25 more, set separately for each profile. It changes how amounts look; no exchange rates are applied.

Encrypted backups

Save a JSON backup, or lock it with a password using AES-256. Lose the password and nobody, including the developer, can open it.

PDF statements

Export every entry or choose a date range. Dates include the year, so old statements stay unambiguous.

Installs like an app

It is a Progressive Web App: add it to your home screen and it opens full screen with its own icon. No app store involved.

Safe deletion

Deleting a profile or clearing its data means typing the profile's name first, so a stray tap cannot erase anything.

Open source

Plain JavaScript, no framework and no build step. Read every line on GitHub.

There is no server, so there is nothing to breach.

SpendTrail was built without a backend. Your entries sit in your browser's own storage and go nowhere. That is how it is built, not a promise in a policy.

On your home screen in under a minute.

No app store and nothing to download. Open SpendTrail in your browser, then add it like this.

Android, in Chrome

  1. Open SpendTrail and let it load once.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Confirm. The icon appears on your home screen.

iPhone, in Safari

  1. Open SpendTrail in Safari. Other iPhone browsers cannot install it.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add in the top right.

Desktop browsers

  1. Open SpendTrail in Chrome, Edge or Brave.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  3. Click Install. It opens in its own window.

Questions people ask first.

Is SpendTrail really free?

Yes. It is open source under the MIT licence, with no paid tier, no subscription and no advertising.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device, in your browser's IndexedDB storage. There is no backend server, so your entries are never uploaded.

The first time you open the app it downloads its chart, PDF and encryption libraries from public CDNs and then caches them. Your entries are never part of any request.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. After the first visit a service worker keeps the app on your device, so you can add and review entries offline.

Can I sync between my phone and computer?

Not automatically, because there is no server to sync through. Open More, Backup and Restore, save a backup file, and restore it on the other device.

What happens if I clear my browser data?

Your SpendTrail entries are cleared with it, because they are stored in the browser. Make a backup first, and keep it somewhere safe.

Does it convert currencies?

No. You pick one of 30 currency symbols for each profile and amounts display with it. No exchange rates are used.

Is there a dark mode?

Yes, since version 4.0.17. Go to More, Appearance and choose Light or Dark.

Who makes it?

SpendTrail is built and maintained by Manan Madani. Bug reports and pull requests are welcome on GitHub.
